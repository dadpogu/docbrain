> [!IMPORTANT]
> Complete this check **once per shift before the first lift**, and again whenever the lifting gear is changed. If any item is marked ✗, **do not lift.** Tag the equipment out of service and raise F-004. The crane operator must hold the appropriate High Risk Work Licence where the OHS Regulations 2017, Part 3.6 require one.
