**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft for approval  
**Record Owner:** Facility Manager  
**Retention:** Follow the Records Register and EPA waste tracking requirements **[TBC]**

> [!NOTE]
> Record every waste or by-product that is generated, stored or sent off site. Apply the waste hierarchy (avoid → reduce → reuse → recycle → recover → treat → dispose), as described in the [National Waste Policy 2018](../assets/regulations/National%20Waste%20Policy%202018.md) and FOM-001 §7.10. Use only authorised transporters and receivers, and keep the movement evidence (e.g. EPA waste tracking certificate or transaction number).
