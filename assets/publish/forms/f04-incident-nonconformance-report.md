> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not delay a mandatory notification while you complete this form** (FOM-001 §7.13). For notifiable incidents, phone **WorkSafe Victoria immediately (13 23 60)** and send the written record within **48 hours** (OHS Act 2004 s.38). Preserve the incident site (s.39). For pollution incidents, check the EPA licence notification requirements and notify **EPA Victoria (1300 372 842)** as required.
