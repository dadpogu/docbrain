# Form: Pot Cycle Batch Record
**Form ID:** F-001  
**Related Process:** P-001 Thermolysis Pot Cycle ([p01-high-level-process.md](../processes/p01-high-level-process.md))  
**Related WIs:** WI-001 to WI-010  
**Revision:** 1.1  
**Status:** Draft for approval  
**Record Owner:** Facility Manager  
**Retention:** Follow the Records Register. Suggested minimum is 7 years **[TBC]**, to cover the licence, excise and customer traceability requirements.

> [!NOTE]
> Use one form for each pot cycle. This is the master traceability record for the batch: it links the feedstock lots, the pot, the kiln, the program, the outputs and any F-004 reports. Write in pen (or use the controlled electronic version). If you make a mistake, strike it through once, then initial and date the correction. Do not use correction fluid.
