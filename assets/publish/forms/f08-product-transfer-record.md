# Form: Product Transfer Record
**Form ID:** F-008  
**Related Process:** P-001 Thermolysis Pot Cycle ([p01-high-level-process.md](../processes/p01-high-level-process.md))  
**Related WIs:** WI-010 (and WI-003 for H4 drawn into the next batch)  
**Revision:** 1.1  
**Status:** Draft for approval  
**Record Owner:** Facility Manager / Quality Analyst  
**Retention:** Follow the Records Register **[TBC]**. Product stock and movement records may also be needed for excise purposes (Excise Act 1901 s.50; **applicability to be confirmed**).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Transfer **one fraction at a time**. **H1, H2 and H3 go to filtration only. H4 is never pumped to filtration;** it is kept for reprocessing. A second person must verify the line-up before the pump starts. Stay with the transfer the whole time.
