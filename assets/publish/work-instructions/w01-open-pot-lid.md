## 1. Purpose & Scope
**Purpose:** To remove the pot lid safely after a thermolysis cycle without causing ignition, uncontrolled release of process gases or odour, or damage to the lid sensors.

**Scope:** Covers the period from when the pot arrives in the Cleaning Area (see [WI-009](w09-lifting-the-pot.md)) to when the lid is resting on wooden pallets. Pot cleaning is covered by [WI-002](w02-clean-the-pot.md).

## 2. Safety & PPE (Critical for Pyrolysis)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DANGER:** Hot surfaces, residual flammable or toxic gases (hydrocarbon vapour, CO, H₂S) and pyrophoric char. **Do not loosen any nut until the pot or lid temperature is < 60 °C and the gas test has passed.** Before starting, confirm that the area's LEL (Lower Explosive Limit) sensors are working.

- **Required PPE:** Heat-resistant gloves, safety glasses and face shield, flame-resistant (FR) coveralls, steel-capped boots, hearing protection (pneumatic tools), half-face respirator with combined organic vapour/P2 cartridges when the lid is lifted, and a personal 4-gas detector (LEL, O₂, CO, H₂S).
- **Emergency Stop:** Use the RED emergency stop button on the main control panel. Other emergency stop locations: **[TBC]**. For an emergency response, follow [WI-007](w07-emergency-shutdown.md).
- **Key hazards:** burns, gas release, ignition from mechanical sparks, suspended loads, pinch points between the lid and rim, compressed-air hose whip, and noise.
- **Ignition control:** Use only pneumatic tools. Do not use electrical power tools, grinders or open flames. Any hot work needs a hot-work permit. Use non-sparking (or inspected, impact-rated) sockets.
- **Lifting:** Crane operators must hold a High Risk Work Licence where required by the OHS Regulations 2017, Part 3.6. Nobody may stand under a suspended lid.

## 3. Environmental Aspects & Controls (ISO 14001)
| Aspect | Potential Impact | Control |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| Fugitive gas or odour when the lid seal is broken | Air pollution, odour complaints from neighbours, licence breach | Before loosening, connect the extraction hood to the activated carbon system and run it. If gas or odour is detected, stop work. |
| Grease or sealant residue | Soil or stormwater contamination | Collect the residue in a labelled oily-waste bin and keep it out of drains. |
| Compressed air use and noise | Energy use and noise at the site boundary | Repair air leaks and isolate the air supply when it is not in use. Work only during approved operating hours. |
| Char dust from the open pot | Dust emission and fire | Keep extraction running and move promptly to WI-002. |

## 6. Pre-conditions (Start Criteria)
- [ ] The pot has been moved to the Cleaning Area under [WI-009](w09-lifting-the-pot.md). All sensor cables are disconnected and the column flange is blanked.
- [ ] The Pot Cycle Batch Record (F-001) for this pot is open and earlier steps are signed.
- [ ] The activated carbon system is available and its media is within its change-out interval.
- [ ] The crane pre-use check (F-002) is complete.

## 7. Step-by-Step Procedure
| Step | Action | Critical Check / Quality Gate |
|------|--------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Confirm the pot ID and batch number match F-001. | Pot ID matches the batch record. |
| 2 | Measure the lid and pot shell temperature at a minimum of **3** points (top of lid, pot shell, rim). Record the highest reading. | **HOLD POINT:** the highest reading must be **< 60 °C**. If it is ≥ 60 °C, wait and re-measure every 30 min. Record each reading. |
| 3 | Position the extraction hood over the lid/rim joint and the column outlet. Start the activated carbon system fan. | Extraction is running and airflow is confirmed (indicator or tell-tale). |
| 4 | Gas-test at the lid/rim joint and the column outlet. | LEL **< 10 %**, O₂ **19.5–23.5 %**, CO **< [TBC] ppm**, H₂S **< [TBC] ppm**. Record on F-003. Do not proceed if any limit is exceeded. |
| 5 | Inspect the pneumatic tool, hose, couplings and whip checks. Set the regulator to deliver torque **x Nm [TBC]**. | The tool is in good condition and the setting matches the tag on the tool. |
| 6 | **First pass:** loosen all 30 nuts by about half a turn in a star (cross) pattern. | The lid releases evenly. Watch the gas detector continuously. |
| 7 | **If gas, fumes or a foul odour appear at any time:** stop work. Keep extraction running so the gases go to the activated carbon system. Step back upwind and re-test. | Resume only when the readings are back within the Step 4 limits and the Facility Manager or delegate approves. Record on F-003. |
| 8 | **Second pass:** remove all 30 nuts in the same star pattern. Place them in the labelled nut container. | Count = **30**. Quarantine-tag any nut with damaged threads and replace it. |
| 9 | Fit rated lifting gear to the lid lifting points. Set an exclusion zone. | Lifting gear is tagged and in date. No personnel are inside the exclusion zone. |
| 10 | Lift the lid about **100 mm** and pause. If the sealant is holding the lid, free it with non-sparking wedges. Never use steel bars. | The lid is free and level. Gas readings are acceptable. |
| 11 | Lift the lid clear and move it to the Lid Rest Area. Lower it onto the wooden pallets so that the sensors on the underside and inside do **not** touch the floor or pallet edges. | The sensors are protected and the lid is stable on the pallets. |
| 12 | Visually inspect the lid sensors, cable connectors and sealing face. | Record any damage on F-001. If a sensor is damaged, tag it out and raise F-004. |
| 13 | Leave extraction running over the open pot and proceed to [WI-002](w02-clean-the-pot.md). | Sign off WI-001 in F-001. |

## 8. Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| Temperature stays ≥ 60 °C | Large char mass or high ambient temperature | Keep waiting. Do not force-cool with water, because steam and thermal shock can damage the pot. Notify the Facility Manager if cooling exceeds **[TBC] h**. |
| Gas detector alarms | Residual hydrocarbon or CO in the pot | Stop, keep extraction on and withdraw. Re-test after **[TBC] min**. If the alarm persists, start [WI-007](w07-emergency-shutdown.md) and raise F-004. |
| Odour noticed outside the building | Extraction not capturing, or carbon media saturated | Stop work and check the extraction and carbon media. Record a possible complaint or incident on F-004 (FOM-001 §7.13). |
| Nut seized | Corrosion, or thermally bonded sealant | Use approved penetrating lubricant only (check the SDS for flammability). Do not apply heat or grinding unless a hot-work permit is issued. |
| Lid will not release | Sealant bonded to the rim | Use non-sparking wedges evenly around the rim. Do not over-load the crane. |
| Sensor damaged | Contact during lift or set-down | Tag it out, raise F-004 and notify the Facility Manager to arrange maintenance. Replace and calibrate the sensor before [WI-005](w05-place-the-pot.md). |
