## 1. Purpose & Scope
**Purpose:** To seal the loaded pot gas-tight, so that no process gas or vapour can leak during thermolysis and no air can enter the pot. A good seal protects product quality, prevents fugitive emissions and odour, and protects workers.

**Scope:** Starts with a loaded pot ([WI-003](w03-load-the-pot.md)). Ends with the lid torqued to specification and the pot ready to move to the kiln ([WI-005](w05-place-the-pot.md)).

## 2. Safety & PPE (Critical for Pyrolysis)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CRITICAL QUALITY AND SAFETY STEP:** A badly sealed lid is a leading cause of gas leaks, fires and odour events during thermolysis. Follow the torque sequence exactly and do not skip the verification pass.

- **Required PPE:** Safety glasses, nitrile gloves (grease and sealant), FR coveralls, steel-capped boots, hearing protection (pneumatic tool).
- **Emergency Stop:** RED button on the main control panel. Loading area emergency stop: **[TBC location]**. Response: [WI-007](w07-emergency-shutdown.md).
- **Key hazards:** suspended lid, hand crush points between the lid and rim (**never put fingers in bolt holes to align them**; use alignment pins or drift pins), compressed-air hose whip, skin contact with sealant, and noise.

## 3. Environmental Aspects & Controls (ISO 14001)
| Aspect | Potential Impact | Control |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| Leaking lid seal during the process | Fugitive VOC emissions, odour, fire, product loss | Use the correct sealant, the torque sequence and a verification pass. Do a leak check during start-up ([WI-006](w06-start-the-process.md)). |
| Excess grease or sealant, and empty cartridges | Waste generation, soil contamination | Apply only the specified bead. Put empty cartridges and rags in the oily-waste bin. |
| Sealant VOCs | Local air quality | Use a low-VOC product where it meets the specification. Keep the area ventilated. |

## 4. Roles, Responsibilities & Competence
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Operator | Applies the sealant, fits the nuts, torques them and records the result. |
| Crane Operator (HRWL where required) | Lifts and lowers the lid. |
| Second competent person (another Operator or the Facility Manager; not the person who torqued the nuts) | Independently verifies the torque on the final pass (two-person check). |

## 5. Tools & Equipment
- Approved high-temperature grease and sealant **[product TBC; SDS available]** and applicator gun
- Pneumatic nut runner, with regulator set to deliver torque **x Nm [TBC]**
- Calibrated torque wrench, used for verification (calibration current)
- 30 serviceable nuts (plus new washers or studs if the specification requires them **[TBC]**) and anti-seize compound if specified
- 2 or more tapered alignment or drift pins
- Overhead crane and rated lid lifting gear
- Paint marker for torque witness marks
- Lint-free rags and oily-waste bin

## 6. Pre-conditions (Start Criteria)
- [ ] WI-003 has been completed and signed in F-001.
- [ ] The pot rim and lid sealing face are clean, dry and undamaged.
- [ ] Lid sensors were inspected and are serviceable (or replaced and calibrated after F-004).
- [ ] The torque tool setting has been checked against the tool tag.

## 7. Step-by-Step Procedure
| Step | Action | Critical Check / Quality Gate |
|------|--------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Inspect the lid sealing face, the lid sensors and the column outlet. | Clean, undamaged and unobstructed. |
| 2 | Apply grease and sealant to the pot rim as a continuous bead **[TBC mm]** wide. Run the bead on the inside of the bolt circle. | **Quality gate:** the bead is unbroken all the way round, with no gaps or overlaps. |
| 3 | Complete the crane pre-use check. Lift the lid from the pallets and keep the sensors clear of all edges. | F-002 is complete and the exclusion zone is set. |
| 4 | Lower the lid to about **50 mm** above the rim. Fit the alignment pins in two opposite bolt holes. | Holes are aligned using pins only. Keep hands clear. |
| 5 | Lower the lid fully onto the rim. Check that the sealant squeezes out evenly. | The lid seats evenly with no visible gap. |
| 6 | Fit all 30 nuts hand-tight. Remove the alignment pins and fit the remaining nuts. | Nut count = **30**. Every nut engages freely by hand. |
| 7 | **Pass 1:** run down all nuts to about **30 %** of torque x in a star (cross) pattern. | Lid is pulled down evenly. |
| 8 | **Pass 2:** tighten all nuts to about **70 %** of torque x in a star pattern. | Lid is pulled down evenly. |
| 9 | **Pass 3:** tighten all nuts to **100 % torque x Nm [TBC]** in a star pattern. | All nuts are at full torque. |
| 10 | **Verification pass:** a second competent person checks every nut with the calibrated torque wrench in a clockwise sequence. Apply a paint witness mark to each verified nut. | **HOLD POINT:** 30 of 30 nuts reach torque x without turning. Two signatures are recorded on F-001. |
| 11 | Wipe off excess sealant. Fit the cap to the column outlet to protect it while the pot is moved. Put waste in the oily-waste bin. | Outlet is protected and the area is clean. Sign off WI-004 in F-001. |

## 8. Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| Bolt holes will not align | Lid rotated, or rim distorted | Lift the lid, re-orient it and re-pin. If the rim is distorted, do not force it. Raise F-004 and notify the Facility Manager to arrange maintenance. |
| Nut turns again on the verification pass | Uneven pull-down, or sealant still settling | Repeat Pass 3 in sequence, then repeat the verification pass on all 30 nuts. |
| Nut cross-threads or galls | Damaged stud or nut | Remove it and replace both the nut and stud (if replaceable). Quarantine-tag the damaged parts. |
| Uneven sealant squeeze-out or a gap | Bead broken, or rim contaminated | Lift the lid, clean both faces, re-apply sealant and repeat from Step 3. |
| Torque tool output differs from the torque wrench | Regulator drift, or tool fault | Tag the tool out of service and use a calibrated tool. Raise F-004 (calibration nonconformity, FOM-001 §6.2). |
