# Work Instruction: Load the Pot
**WI ID:** WI-003  
**Related Process:** P-001 Thermolysis Pot Cycle ([p01-high-level-process.md](../processes/p01-high-level-process.md))  
**Revision:** 1.1  
**Location:** Loading Area  
**Document Owner:** Facility Manager  
**Approver:** Operations Director  
**Status:** Draft for approval  
**Effective Date:** To be assigned on approval  
**Next Review:** 12 months after approval, or earlier following material change or incident

> [!NOTE]
> Values marked **[TBC]** must be confirmed before approval. Batch mass, fill level and H4 ratio must match the approved recipe for the selected thermolysis program.

## 1. Purpose & Scope
**Purpose:** To load the pot with accepted, traceable plastic feedstock and the specified quantity of recycled H4 liquid, so that the batch is consistent, product quality is predictable, and the feedstock lots are linked to the batch record.

**Scope:** Starts with a clean, inspected pot ([WI-002](w02-clean-the-pot.md)). Ends with a loaded pot that is ready for lid closure ([WI-004](w04-close-pot-lid.md)). Covers pre-baled and shredded plastic from bulk bags, and H4 liquid from previous cycles.

## 2. Safety & PPE (Critical for Pyrolysis)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DANGER:** H4 liquid is a combustible hydrocarbon. Keep ignition sources away, ground and bond the transfer equipment, and use drip trays. Suspended bulk bags can fall: **never stand under a suspended bag or reach under it to open the discharge spout.** Use the spout access from the side.

- **Required PPE:** Safety glasses, nitrile gloves (H4 handling), cut-resistant gloves (strapping), FR coveralls, steel-capped boots, a P2 dust mask when discharging shredded plastic, and hearing protection if required.
- **Emergency Stop:** RED button on the main control panel. Loading area emergency stop: **[TBC location]**. Response: [WI-007](w07-emergency-shutdown.md).
- **Key hazards:** suspended loads, manual handling of bales, strapping recoil and cuts, plastic dust, H4 skin contact and vapour, and slips from spills.

## 3. Environmental Aspects & Controls (ISO 14001)
| Aspect | Potential Impact | Control |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| Plastic dust or fragments escaping during discharge | Litter, stormwater contamination | Discharge slowly with the spout inside the pot mouth. Clean up loose plastic immediately. |
| H4 transfer spill | Soil or stormwater contamination, vapour | Transfer in the bunded area using drip trays, and supervise the transfer the whole time. Keep the spill kit at hand. Keep drain covers in place. |
| Empty bulk bags and strapping | Waste generation | Fold the bags for reuse or return to the supplier if they are serviceable. Otherwise segregate them for recycling. Put metal strapping in the scrap metal bin. |
| Prohibited or contaminated feedstock | Off-specification product, harmful emissions (e.g. HCl from PVC), licence breach | Load only lots with **Accepted** status (FOM-001 §7.5). Do a visual check at discharge. |

## 4. Roles, Responsibilities & Competence
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Operator | Confirms feedstock status, loads the pot, transfers H4, and records lots and quantities. |
| Crane / Forklift Operator (HRWL where required) | Lifts the pot and bulk bags. |
| Facility Manager or delegate | Authorises the batch recipe and decides what to do with suspect feedstock. |

## 5. Tools & Equipment
- Overhead crane, bulk bag lifting frame, rated slings
- Forklift (for bales and bags), with a licensed operator
- Calibrated weighing system (crane load cell or platform scale)
- H4 transfer pump, earthed hose with bonding clamp, drip trays
- Strapping cutter with a safety blade
- Fill-level gauge or dipstick, marked at the maximum fill height
- Spill kit, waste bins (plastic film, scrap metal, general waste)

## 6. Pre-conditions (Start Criteria)
- [ ] WI-002 has been completed and the hold point signed in F-001.
- [ ] The Facility Manager has authorised a batch sheet (recipe and target quantities) (FOM-001 §7.6).
- [ ] Feedstock lots are tagged **Accepted** and are compatible with the recipe.
- [ ] H4 is available in the identified H4 tank and its volume is recorded.

## 7. Step-by-Step Procedure
| Step | Action | Critical Check / Quality Gate |
|------|--------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Complete the crane pre-use check. Lift the clean pot and place it in the Loading Area on the locating stand. | F-002 is complete and the pot is seated level. |
| 2 | Identify the feedstock lots for the batch. Check the status tag and lot ID of each bag or bale against the batch sheet. | **Quality gate:** only lots tagged **Accepted** and listed on the batch sheet. Record the lot IDs on F-001. |
| 3 | Weigh each bulk bag or bale (or use the verified receipt weight). | Running total ≤ target batch mass of **[TBC] kg**. |
| 4 | **Bulk bags:** lift the bag over the pot. Position the spout inside the pot mouth. Open the bottom discharge from the side and control the flow. | Discharge is controlled and no plastic escapes. |
| 5 | **Pre-baled plastic:** place the bales in the pot. Remove all strapping or wire before placing (unless the recipe allows it **[TBC]**). | No metal strapping is left in the pot. |
| 6 | While discharging, watch for prohibited items (e.g. PVC, metal, glass, liquids, batteries, other materials **[TBC per approved waste stream list]**). | If you see a suspect item, stop. Remove it if safe, quarantine it, and raise F-004. |
| 7 | Check the fill level. | Fill height ≤ **[TBC] mm below the rim**, so the charge cannot touch the lid sensors or block the column. |
| 8 | Earth and bond the H4 pump and hose. Place drip trays under the connections. Transfer H4 from the H4 tank into the pot. | Volume = **[TBC] L** per the recipe. Record the H4 tank ID and the before and after levels. |
| 9 | Disconnect the hose over the drip tray and cap the hose ends. Wipe any H4 off the rim. | The rim is clean and dry, ready for WI-004. |
| 10 | Fold empty bulk bags for reuse or recycling. Segregate strapping and waste. Clean up loose plastic. | The area is clean and waste is segregated. |
| 11 | Record the total feedstock mass, lot IDs, H4 volume and fill height. | Sign off WI-003 in F-001. |

## 8. Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| Bulk bag will not discharge | Material compacted or bridged | Close the spout, lower the bag, and agitate it from the side. Do not reach under the suspended bag. |
| Target mass reached before the fill level (or the reverse) | Feedstock density differs from the recipe assumption | Stop at whichever limit comes first. Record it and notify the Facility Manager for recipe review. |
| Prohibited material found | Supplier contamination | Quarantine the material and the affected lot. Raise F-004 and notify the supplier (FOM-001 §7.4, §7.9). |
| H4 spill | Hose or coupling failure | Stop the pump, contain with the spill kit, and protect the drains. Record on F-004. |
| Not enough H4 available | Previous yield low, or H4 used elsewhere | Do not substitute another fraction. Hold the batch and notify the Facility Manager. |
