# Work Instruction: Lifting the Pot from the Kiln

**Approver:** Operations Director

## 1. Purpose & Scope
**Purpose:** To disconnect the cooled pot from the condenser and move it, with the lid still on, to the Cleaning Area. Residual gas must not escape, and the pot, lid sensors and lifting equipment must not be damaged.

**Scope:** Starts when the pot has cooled to ≤ 100 °C after [WI-008](w08-stopping-the-process.md). Ends with the pot in the Cleaning Area, ready for [WI-001](w01-open-pot-lid.md) (the lid is removed only once the pot is below 60 °C).

## 2. Safety & PPE (Critical for Pyrolysis)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DANGER:** The pot is at up to **100 °C**, so contact causes serious burns. The column outlet can release residual vapour when the flange is opened. **Use pneumatic tools only (no sparks).** The suspended load is heavy (**[TBC kg]**). Nobody may stand under it.

- **Required PPE:** Heat-resistant gloves (rated ≥ 100 °C contact), safety glasses and face shield, FR coveralls, steel-capped boots, hard hat, hearing protection, personal 4-gas detector.
- **Emergency Stop:** RED button on the main control panel. Crane pendant emergency stop. Response: [WI-007](w07-emergency-shutdown.md).
- **Key hazards:** hot surfaces, residual gas, suspended load, a hot pot moving through the building, and noise from pneumatic tools.

## 3. Environmental Aspects & Controls (ISO 14001)
| Aspect | Potential Impact | Control |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| Vapour released when the column flange is opened | Odour, VOC emissions | Confirm the knife valves are closed. Open the flange under local extraction routed to the activated carbon system. Blank both flanges immediately. |
| Tar or condensate drips from the flange | Soil contamination | Place a drip tray under the flange. Put any residue in the oily-waste bin. |
| Used gasket | Waste | Discard it to the oily or contaminated waste stream. Never reuse it. |

## 4. Roles, Responsibilities & Competence
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Operator | Disconnects the sensors, opens the flange and fits the blanks. |
| Crane Operator (HRWL where required) | Lifts and moves the pot. |
| Dogger / Rigger (if required) | Slings the load and directs the crane. |

## 5. Tools & Equipment
- Pneumatic nut remover (no electric tools)
- Calibrated infrared thermometer or the panel pot thermocouple
- Personal 4-gas detector and local extraction hood
- Flange blanks (pot column side and condenser side) with gaskets
- Drip tray, oily-waste bin
- Overhead crane, rated hooks and lifting gear (heat-rated if in contact with hot surfaces)
- Sensor connector dust caps

## 6. Pre-conditions (Start Criteria)
- [ ] WI-008 has been completed and signed in F-001.
- [ ] The kiln is de-energised and isolated (LOTO applied).
- [ ] The Cleaning Area is clear and ready to receive the pot.

## 7. Step-by-Step Procedure
| Step | Action | Critical Check / Quality Gate |
|------|--------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Measure the pot temperature (panel thermocouple, confirmed with the IR thermometer). | **HOLD POINT:** pot temperature ≤ **100 °C**. Record it on F-001. |
| 2 | Confirm that the column flange knife valve and the hot condenser knife valve are **closed**. | Both indicators show CLOSED, checked against F-006. |
| 3 | Disconnect all sensor cables from the pot lid. Fit dust caps to the connectors. | All cables are disconnected, capped and stowed clear of the lift path. |
| 4 | Position the extraction hood and the drip tray at the column flange. Gas-test. | Extraction is running and LEL < 10 %. |
| 5 | Remove the column flange nuts with the pneumatic nut remover, working in a star pattern. | No vapour release. If there is gas or odour, stop and follow Troubleshooting. |
| 6 | Separate the flanges. Remove and discard the used gasket. Fit blanks to **both** the pot column flange and the condenser flange right away. | Both openings are blanked. |
| 7 | Complete the crane pre-use check. Attach the hooks to the pot lid lifting points per the approved lifting plan. | F-002 is complete. Hooks are attached as the lifting plan specifies **[TBC: confirm with manufacturer that lid lugs are rated for full pot mass]**. |
| 8 | Lift the pot and the lid out of the kiln and set an exclusion zone along the travel path. | The lift is smooth and level, and the path is clear of people. |
| 9 | Move the pot to the Cleaning Area and lower it onto the stand. | The pot is stable. Place "HOT" signage on or near it. |
| 10 | Remove the lifting gear. Remove your lock from the kiln if no other work is pending. | Sign off WI-009 in F-001. Proceed to WI-001 once the pot is < 60 °C. |

## 8. Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| Pot temperature stays > 100 °C | Large char mass, or insulated kiln | Keep waiting. Do not force-cool. Record the temperature every **[TBC] h**. |
| Vapour or odour when the flange is opened | Knife valve passing, or residual gas in the pot | Re-tighten the flange nuts, keep extraction on, and re-check the valve positions. Raise F-004. |
| Flange nuts seized | Heat, or tar bonding | Use approved penetrating lubricant once the pot is cool. Do not use heat or grinding without a hot-work permit. |
| Pot catches or binds in the kiln when lifted | Misalignment, or debris | Stop the lift and lower the pot. Re-centre the crane hook over the pot. Do not drag the pot. |
| Lid lugs show deformation | Overload, or fatigue | Stop. Do not lift. Raise F-004 and get an engineering inspection. |
