## 1. Purpose & Scope
**Purpose:** To remove grease, sealant and char residue from the pot so that the next batch is not contaminated, the lid seals reliably, and the char is captured, identified and managed as a controlled output.

**Scope:** Starts with the lid removed ([WI-001](w01-open-pot-lid.md)). Ends with a clean, inspected pot that is ready for loading ([WI-003](w03-load-the-pot.md)). Covers rim cleaning, char removal in the enclosed cleaning bay, and transfer of char to storage.

## 2. Safety & PPE (Critical for Pyrolysis)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **DANGER:** Freshly produced char can be **pyrophoric**: it can self-heat or ignite on contact with air. Char dust is a respiratory and combustible-dust hazard. **Never enter the pot.** It is a confined space (OHS Regulations 2017, Part 3.4), and all cleaning is done from outside using the scraper and suction system.

- **Required PPE:** Safety glasses and face shield, nitrile gloves under cut-resistant gloves, FR coveralls (disposable over-suit for char work), steel-capped boots, hearing protection, and a half-face respirator with combined organic vapour/P2 cartridges (a P3 powered air-purifying respirator (PAPR) if specified by the dust assessment **[TBC]**).
- **Emergency Stop:** RED button on the main control panel. Cleaning bay scraper/suction emergency stop: **[TBC location]**. Response: [WI-007](w07-emergency-shutdown.md).
- **Key hazards:** char dust, self-heating char, rotating scraper, vacuum suction points, suspended pot, solvent vapour, and noise.
- Keep a CO₂ or dry-powder extinguisher and a lidded metal char bin at the cleaning bay.

## 3. Environmental Aspects & Controls (ISO 14001)
| Aspect | Potential Impact | Control |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| Char dust release | Air quality, neighbour amenity, worker health | Clean only inside the enclosed bay with the doors closed. The suction must be running **before** the scraper starts. Inspect the suction filter every shift. |
| Char residue (solid waste or by-product) | Unlawful disposal, resource loss, contamination | Bag the char, label it, weigh it and record it in the waste register. Store it covered and segregated. Send it only to an approved receiver or approved beneficial use, following the waste hierarchy (National Waste Policy 2018). |
| Grease, sealant and solvent-soaked rags | Soil or stormwater contamination, VOC emissions | Use a minimum quantity of approved solvent. Put waste in a labelled, lidded oily-waste bin. Keep it out of drains. |
| Electricity use (scraper and vacuum) | Resource use | Stop the equipment when it is not in use. |

## 6. Pre-conditions (Start Criteria)
- [ ] WI-001 has been completed and signed in F-001.
- [ ] Pot temperature is **< 60 °C**.
- [ ] Cleaning bay suction filters and collection bags are serviceable, with capacity available.
- [ ] There is capacity in the Char Storage Area.

## 7. Step-by-Step Procedure
| Step | Action | Critical Check / Quality Gate |
|------|--------|------------------------------|
| 1 | Scrape the old grease and sealant off the pot rim with a non-sparking scraper. Wipe the rim with rags and a minimum of solvent. Put the waste in the oily-waste bin. | No residue is left on the sealing face. |
| 2 | Inspect the rim sealing face and bolt holes. | **Quality gate:** no nicks, pits, dents or damaged threads. If you find a defect, raise F-004 and notify the Facility Manager to arrange maintenance before loading. |
| 3 | Complete the crane pre-use check. Attach the rated lifting gear to the pot lifting lugs. | F-002 is complete and the exclusion zone is set. |
| 4 | Lift the pot with the crane and place it in the cleaning bay on the locating stand. Close the enclosure doors. | The pot is seated and the doors are closed and interlocked. |
| 5 | Fit an empty char bag or container to the suction discharge. Pre-label it with the batch number, date and "CHAR – [TBC waste code]". | The label is legible and the container is empty. |
| 6 | Start the **suction first**, then start the scraper. | The suction is running before the scraper starts. No dust is visible outside the enclosure. |
| 7 | Run the scraper cycle (**[TBC] min**). Monitor the char temperature at the discharge with the IR thermometer. | If the char is **> [TBC] °C** or smouldering, stop and follow the Troubleshooting section. |
| 8 | Stop the scraper. Let the suction run for **[TBC] min** to clear the remaining dust, then stop it. | The enclosure air is clear before the doors are opened. |
| 9 | Inspect the inside of the pot **from outside** with a torch or borescope. | **HOLD POINT:** no remaining char deposits, cracks, bulging or heavy corrosion. Signed by the Quality Analyst (or the Facility Manager). |
| 10 | Seal the char containers. Weigh them and record the net char mass. Take a char sample if the sampling plan requires one. | The mass is recorded on F-001 (for the mass balance) and on the Waste Tracking Register (F-005). |
| 11 | Move the sealed char to the Char Storage Area: covered, segregated, and away from ignition sources and combustibles. | The container is labelled and its status is shown (awaiting test, released, or for disposal). |
| 12 | Housekeeping: vacuum any spilt char (do not dry-sweep), empty the oily-waste bin if it is full, and check the filters. | The area is clean. Sign off WI-002 in F-001. |

## 8. Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Corrective Action |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| Char smouldering or hot | Pyrophoric char exposed to air | Close the enclosure and stop the scraper. Keep the char contained in a lidded metal container. Use an extinguisher if safe. If the fire grows, follow [WI-007](w07-emergency-shutdown.md). Raise F-004. |
| Dust visible outside the bay | Filter blocked or torn, or doors open | Stop the scraper, keep the suction running, and replace the filter. Record the possible emission on F-004. |
| Hard char deposit not removed | Over-temperature batch, or feedstock contamination | Repeat one scraper cycle. If the deposit remains, notify the Facility Manager to arrange maintenance (no pot entry). Review the batch record for causes. |
| Rim damage found | Mechanical damage during lid removal | Do not load. Raise F-004. Maintenance (arranged by the Facility Manager) repairs the rim and verifies it before the pot is returned to service. |
| Char storage full | Collection overdue | Stop cleaning. Notify the Facility Manager to arrange collection with an approved receiver. |
