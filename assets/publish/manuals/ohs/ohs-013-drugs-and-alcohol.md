## 1. Purpose
To make sure nobody works while impaired by alcohol or other drugs, because impairment puts the worker and others at serious risk. Our site involves flammable gases and liquids, high temperatures, cranes, forklifts and safety-critical decisions. The policy also aims to support workers who have alcohol or drug problems.

## 2. Scope
Covers all employees, labour-hire workers, contractors and visitors while on site, operating company vehicles or plant, or attending work functions.

## 3. Policy
1. Everyone must present for work and **stay fit for work**, not impaired by alcohol, illicit drugs, or misused prescription or over-the-counter medicines.
2. Possessing, consuming, selling or distributing alcohol or illicit drugs on site is **prohibited**.
3. The standard is **0.00 % BAC** for safety-critical roles: operators, crane and forklift operators, maintenance staff, and drivers. The standard for all other roles is **below 0.02 % BAC** **[TBC – confirm through consultation]**.
4. Workers taking medication that may affect their ability to work safely (e.g. drowsiness warnings) must tell the Facility Manager **before starting work**. They do not need to name the medication. The Facility Manager adjusts their duties.
5. Workers who seek help for alcohol or drug problems **before** an incident or a test will be supported (EAP, leave, adjusted duties), and this will not affect their employment.
6. At work functions where alcohol is served by approval, responsible service applies: food, non-alcoholic drinks, safe transport home, and nobody returns to safety-critical work afterwards.

## 4. Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Operations Director | Provides resources, including the EAP and the testing provider if adopted. |
| Facility Manager | Watches for signs of impairment. Acts on reasonable suspicion. Keeps matters confidential. |
| Workers | Comply with the policy. Disclose medication effects. Report concerns about anyone who appears impaired. |

## 5. Suspected Impairment (Reasonable Suspicion)
Signs include: smell of alcohol, slurred speech, unsteadiness, unusual behaviour, drowsiness, dilated or pinpoint pupils, or erratic driving or plant operation.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | The Facility Manager (or the delegated Operator if the Facility Manager is off site) removes the worker from safety-critical work **immediately** and takes them to a private area. Ask a second person, such as the HR Manager by phone or another manager, to witness if possible. |
| 2 | Talk with the worker respectfully. Consider other causes such as a medical condition, injury, fatigue or medication. **If there is a medical emergency, call 000.** |
| 3 | If testing has been adopted, offer a test (Section 6). |
| 4 | If impairment is suspected or confirmed, the worker does not return to work that shift. **Arrange safe transport home** and do not let them drive. |
| 5 | Record the facts objectively (confidential). Follow up with a meeting, the EAP referral, and any disciplinary process, with procedural fairness. |

## 6. Testing Program [optional; adopt only after consultation]
| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| When | ☐ Pre-employment ☐ Random (computer-generated, minimum **[TBC]**% of workers each year) ☐ Reasonable suspicion ☐ Post-incident (as soon as practicable after a significant incident or near miss) |
| Method | Alcohol: breath test with a calibrated breathalyser (AS 3547). Drugs: oral fluid screening (AS 4760) or urine screening (AS/NZS 4308), done by a trained tester. |
| Non-negative screen | Remove from work. Confirmatory laboratory test from a split sample. Stand down on pay until the result is known **[TBC]**. |
| Confirmed positive | Fitness-for-work meeting. EAP referral. Disciplinary action under the consequence framework below. Return to work only after a negative test. |
| Refusal | Treated as a non-negative result. |
| Privacy | Results go only to the HR Manager (and the Operations Director where action is needed), are stored confidentially, and are not shared without consent except as required by law. |

**Consequence framework [TBC – HR/legal]:** first breach → written warning, EAP, and a return-to-work agreement with follow-up testing. Second breach or serious misconduct (e.g. supplying drugs, or a breach that causes an incident) → may lead to termination.
