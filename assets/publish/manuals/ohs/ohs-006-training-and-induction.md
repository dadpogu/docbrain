## 1. Purpose
To make sure every person who works at or visits the facility gets the information, instruction, training and supervision they need to work safely (OHS Act s.21(2)(e)), and that competence is verified and recorded.

## 2. Scope
Covers employees (new, transferred and returning), labour-hire workers, contractors, visitors and delivery drivers.

## 3. Induction Levels
| Level | Who | Content | Duration | Delivered by |
|-------|-----|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Visitor induction** | Visitors, short-term escorted persons | Site rules, hazards, PPE, emergency alarms, assembly area, escort requirements | 10 min (video/card) | Reception / host |
| **Delivery driver induction** | Feedstock and product transporters | Traffic management, loading and unloading rules, spill response, no smoking or ignition sources, emergency procedures | 15 min | Facility Manager or delegated Operator |
| **Contractor induction** | Contractors working on site | General site induction + permits (hot work, isolation, confined space, work at height), contractor responsibilities, incident reporting | 1 h + task-specific | Facility Manager |
| **General employee induction** | All new employees and labour-hire workers | Appendix A checklist (all items) | 1 day | Facility Manager |
| **Role-specific training** | Operators, maintenance, laboratory staff | Relevant WIs (WI-001 to WI-010), equipment, competency assessment, for each function the Operator will perform | Per Training Matrix | Facility Manager / competent trainer |

Nobody may start work until the relevant induction is complete and signed.

## 4. Training Needs Analysis and Training Matrix
The Facility Manager maintains a **Training and Competency Matrix** (FOM-001 §6.4) that lists the requirements for each role and each Operator function. Operators are multi-skilled, so the Matrix records which functions each Operator is authorised to perform, such as Control Panel, Field, Crane, First Aid or Warden, and any written delegations from the Facility Manager. The HR Manager keeps the training records. Minimum requirements:

| Training / licence | Required for | Refresher |
|--------------------|--------------|-----------|
| General site induction | Everyone | Every 2 years, or after major change |
| WI-001 to WI-010 competency | Operators (per WI) | Every 2 years, or after a WI revision |
| High Risk Work Licence: crane (class per crane type **[TBC]**), forklift (LF), dogging (DG) | Operators authorised for crane, forklift or dogging work (OHS Regs 2017 Part 3.6) | Renew the licence every 5 years |
| Gas detector use and confined space awareness | Operators, maintenance | Annually |
| Hazardous substances and dangerous goods handling ([OHS-015](ohs-015-chemical-management.md)) | Operators, laboratory staff | Every 2 years |
| Manual handling ([OHS-017](ohs-017-manual-handling.md)) | All operational staff | Every 2 years |
| Isolation / LOTO and permit to work | Operators, maintenance | Every 2 years |
| Fire extinguisher and emergency response | All staff | Annually (with drills) |
| Fire / Chief Warden training | Facility Manager (Chief Warden) and Operators appointed as wardens | Every 2 years |
| First aid (HLTAID011 Provide first aid; CPR HLTAID009) | First Aid Officers ([OHS-014](ohs-014-first-aid-arrangements.md)) | First aid every 3 years; CPR annually |
| HSR initial (5 days) and annual refresher | Elected HSRs (OHS Act s.67) | Annually |
| RTW Coordinator training | RTW Coordinator (HR Manager) | Recommended once, then ongoing |
| Psychosocial hazards, bullying and harassment, respectful workplace | All staff; leadership module for managers | Every 2 years |
| Mental health first aid (recommended) | Facility Manager, contact officers | Every 3 years |
| Electrical safety awareness; test & tag (if done in-house) | All staff; competent person | Every 2 years |

## 5. Procedure
1. **Before starting:** the HR Manager or the Facility Manager checks licences and qualifications (sighting the originals and recording them) and schedules the induction.
2. **Day 1:** deliver the general induction using the Appendix A checklist. The worker and the inductor sign it.
3. **Weeks 1–4:** role-specific training **under direct supervision**. The worker does not work unsupervised on any WI until assessed competent.
4. **Competency assessment:** a competent assessor observes the task, asks questions and reviews records. The result is recorded in the Training Matrix.
5. **Ongoing:** refresher training per Section 4, toolbox talks **[TBC – weekly]**, and retraining after incidents, WI changes or poor performance.
6. **Effectiveness check:** the Facility Manager observes work practices. Training effectiveness is reviewed at the 3-month probation review and through inspections and incident trends.
7. **Language and literacy:** deliver training in a form each worker can understand (plain English, visual aids, interpreters where needed).

## 6. Records
Signed induction checklists, training attendance, competency assessments, licence copies and the Training Matrix. Keep for the duration of employment plus **[TBC – 7] years**.
