## 1. Purpose
To provide adequate first aid facilities, trained first aid officers and procedures for this facility (OHS Act s.21(2)(d)), following the WorkSafe Victoria *Compliance code: First aid in the workplace*.

## 5. First Aid Procedure
1. **D**anger: make sure the area is safe. Don't enter gas or fire areas, and use [WI-007](../../work-instructions/w07-emergency-shutdown.md) if needed.
2. **R**esponse → **S**end for help: radio or phone a first aid officer and call **000** for serious injuries. Give the address: **[TBC site address and gate number]**.
3. **A**irway, **B**reathing, **C**PR, **D**efibrillation (AED in the control room).
4. **Specific hazards at this site:**

| Injury | First aid |
|--------|-----------|
| **Thermal burn** | Cool under running water for **20 minutes**. Remove jewellery and clothing that is not stuck to the burn. Cover with cling film or a burn dressing. Don't use ice. Call 000 for burns larger than a palm, or burns to the face, hands, genitals or airway, or full-thickness burns. |
| **Hydrocarbon (H1–H4) on skin** | Remove contaminated clothing (it is a fire risk; keep it away from ignition sources). Wash with soap and water for 15 minutes. Check the SDS. |
| **Chemical or hydrocarbon in the eye** | Use the eyewash for at least **15 minutes** with eyelids held open. Seek medical attention. Take the SDS to hospital. |
| **Inhalation (gas, CO, H₂S, vapour)** | **Don't enter the area without gas testing or breathing apparatus.** Move the person to fresh air if it is safe to do so. Give oxygen if trained and available. Call 000. If they are unconscious, start DRSABCD. |
| **Char dust in the eye or inhaled** | Irrigate the eye. Move to fresh air. |
| **Crush injury** | Call 000. Follow ambulance advice on releasing the load. Control bleeding. |
| **Electric shock** | **Isolate the power before touching the person.** Start DRSABCD. Every electric shock needs medical assessment, and an electric shock needing medical treatment is **notifiable** to WorkSafe. |

5. **Report** the incident (F-004, Register of Injuries) and check whether it is **notifiable** ([OHS-004](ohs-004-incident-reporting-and-investigation.md)).
6. Make sure the injured person gets home or to medical care safely. Start return to work support ([OHS-005](ohs-005-injury-management-and-return-to-work.md)).
