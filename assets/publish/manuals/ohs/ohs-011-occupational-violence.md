## 1. Policy Statement
Aggression and violence toward anyone at work is never acceptable and is **not "part of the job"**. Reclaim Resources will identify and control the risks of work-related violence, support anyone affected, and take action against perpetrators, including reporting to police.

## 2. Scope
Covers aggression or violence toward workers by members of the public, neighbours, feedstock suppliers, transport drivers, customers, contractors, intruders, or other workers, at the facility or while working off-site.

## 3. Definition
**Aggression or violence:** incidents in which a person is abused, threatened or assaulted in circumstances relating to their work (WorkSafe Victoria). Examples: verbal abuse, yelling or swearing, threatening gestures or messages, intimidation, throwing objects, pushing, physical or sexual assault, and use of a vehicle to threaten someone.

## 4. Risk Profile for This Site
| Scenario | Risk factors |
|----------|--------------|
| A driver's load is rejected or refused at the gate (prohibited or contaminated feedstock) | Financial loss to the driver, time pressure, frustration |
| A neighbour or community member complains about odour or traffic in person | High emotion; may arrive unannounced |
| A customer disputes product quality or a delivery | Commercial conflict |
| An intruder or theft at night (fuel products, metals) | Isolated night operator; valuable product on site |
| Worker-to-worker conflict | Long shifts, fatigue, small crews (see also [OHS-009](ohs-009-bullying-and-harassment.md)) |
| Family violence that comes into the workplace | A perpetrator attends the site or calls repeatedly |

## 5. Controls
Follow the risk management process in [OHS-010](ohs-010-psychosocial-risk-management.md) and record it in [F-009](../../forms/f09-psychosocial-prevention-plan.md). Altering the work and environment must be the **predominant** control; training supports it (r.15).

| Control type | Controls |
|--------------|----------|
| **Workplace environment** | Secure perimeter fencing and gate with intercom and camera. Drivers stay at the weighbridge or office counter with a barrier, not in operational areas. Good lighting. CCTV on gate, weighbridge, tank farm and car park, with signs. A lockable control room as a safe retreat. Clear exit routes. |
| **Plant** | Duress alarms or lone-worker devices for the night operator and weighbridge staff **[TBC]**. Phones and radios on every shift. |
| **Systems of work** | Pre-booked deliveries only. Acceptance criteria sent to suppliers **in advance** so rejections are not a surprise. Rejection decisions delivered by the supervisor, not junior staff. Contested rejections referred to the Facility Manager by phone, not argued at the gate. Neighbour complaints handled by appointment or through the complaints line (FOM-001 §7.13). No cash on site. Two-person rule for confrontational situations where practicable. |
| **Management of work** | Lone-worker check-ins on night and weekend shifts. Escalation and police call-out thresholds agreed with the local police **[TBC]**. Family violence safety plans for affected workers (with consent). |
| **Information, instruction, training (supporting)** | Induction, conflict de-escalation training for gate and weighbridge staff and the Facility Manager, and training on reporting and emergency response. |

## 6. Responding to an Incident
| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Protect yourself first.** Do not argue or try to physically restrain anyone. Move to a safe place (control room or office) and lock the door if needed. |
| 2 | Use the duress alarm or radio, and call **000** if there is a threat of harm. |
| 3 | Tell the Facility Manager (or the delegated Operator if the Facility Manager is off site). They make the area safe, stops deliveries if needed, and makes sure nobody is working alone. |
| 4 | Give first aid and medical help ([OHS-014](ohs-014-first-aid-arrangements.md)). Offer psychological first aid and the EAP ([OHS-008](ohs-008-mental-health-and-support-program.md)). |
| 5 | Report the incident on F-004. Keep CCTV footage. Report assaults to police. Notify WorkSafe if it is a notifiable incident (e.g. the person needs in-patient hospital treatment) ([OHS-004](ohs-004-incident-reporting-and-investigation.md)). |
| 6 | Investigate, review controls (an r.16 trigger), and consider a site ban or a supplier or contractor sanction. |
| 7 | Support the worker: time off, check-ins, and help with police processes, workers compensation ([OHS-005](ohs-005-injury-management-and-return-to-work.md)) and victims' support services. |
