## 1. Purpose
To prevent electric shock, burns, arc flash, and fire or explosion caused by electrical sources. The kiln heaters, pumps, control panel and switchboards operate in areas where **flammable gases and vapours** may be present.

## 2. Scope
Covers all electrical installations, equipment, portable tools and leads, and electrical work at the facility, including in hazardous areas.

## 3. Key Rules
1. **Only licensed electricians** (Registered Electrical Contractor and A-grade licence holder, under the *Electricity Safety Act 1998* (Vic)) may do electrical installation work, repairs or fault-finding. **No unlicensed electrical work.**
2. **Never work on live equipment.** Electrical work is done de-energised, isolated, locked, tagged and **tested for dead**. Live work is permitted only in strictly limited circumstances (testing or fault-finding), with a written risk assessment, a safety observer, and a permit.
3. Everyone must follow the site **isolation / lock-out-tag-out (LOTO)** procedure before work on plant, including the kiln ([WI-005](../../work-instructions/w05-place-the-pot.md), [WI-009](../../work-instructions/w09-lifting-the-pot.md)).
4. Only use electrical equipment that is **certified for the zone** in hazardous areas ([OHS-015](ohs-015-chemical-management.md) §4.6). **Do not take personal electronics, non-Ex torches or phones into Zone 1 areas [TBC per classification].**
5. Report any electric shock, however minor. Electric shock requiring medical treatment, or exposure to a serious risk of shock, is **notifiable to WorkSafe** (OHS Act s.37). Serious electrical incidents must also be reported to **Energy Safe Victoria (ESV)** **[TBC – confirm reporting obligations]**.

## 4. Controls
| Hazard | Controls |
|--------|----------|
| Electric shock from portable equipment | **RCDs** (safety switches) on all socket outlets (AS/NZS 3000). Portable RCDs where fixed protection is not available. Inspect, test and tag portable equipment (AS/NZS 3760). Visual check before each use. Remove damaged equipment immediately. |
| Fixed installation faults | Installation by a licensed electrician to AS/NZS 3000, with a Certificate of Electrical Safety. Periodic inspection and thermographic survey of switchboards every **[TBC – 12 months]**. |
| Ignition of flammable atmospheres | Hazardous area classification. Ex-rated equipment. Inspections under AS/NZS 60079.17 by a competent Ex person. Earthing and bonding of tanks, transfer equipment and pipework. Static control. |
| Lightning | Lightning protection system for the tank farm and the building (AS/NZS 1768) **[TBC]**. |
| Arc flash at switchboards | Keep doors closed. Keep 1 m clear access. Arc-flash assessment and PPE for switching **[TBC]**. Switching only by authorised persons. |
| Overhead power lines | No crane or tipper work within the "No Go Zone" distances (WorkSafe/ESV Framework). Spotter required near lines. |
| Underground services | Dial Before You Dig (1100) and service location before excavation. |
| Wet conditions | Keep equipment away from wash-down areas. Use IP-rated equipment outdoors. |
| Emergency | Label emergency isolation points at the main switchboard **[TBC location]**. The RED emergency stop on the control panel shuts down plant power ([WI-007](../../work-instructions/w07-emergency-shutdown.md)). |

## 6. Responding to Electric Shock
1. **Don't touch the person** until the power is isolated (at the switch or the main switchboard).
2. Call **000**. Start DRSABCD and use the AED if needed ([OHS-014](ohs-014-first-aid-arrangements.md)).
3. Every person who receives an electric shock must get a medical assessment.
4. Preserve the scene and notify WorkSafe (and ESV) ([OHS-004](ohs-004-incident-reporting-and-investigation.md)).
