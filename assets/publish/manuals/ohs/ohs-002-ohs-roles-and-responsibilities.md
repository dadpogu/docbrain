## 1. Purpose
To set out who is responsible for what in health and safety at the facility, so that every duty under the OHS Act 2004 has a named owner and every worker knows what is expected of them.

## 2. Scope
Covers all officers, managers, employees, labour-hire workers, contractors and visitors at the Wodonga facility, and any work done off-site on behalf of Reclaim Resources.

## 3. Legal Duties Summary
| Duty holder | Key duties | OHS Act 2004 |
|-------------|------------|--------------|
| Employer (Reclaim Resources) | Provide and maintain a safe working environment without risks to health, so far as reasonably practicable. Extends to contractors in matters the employer controls. | s.21 |
| Employer | Monitor employee health and workplace conditions. Keep information and records. Employ or engage people with suitable OHS expertise to give advice. | s.22 |

## 4. Role Responsibilities
Roles follow the [Organisational Structure](../../assets/docs/Organisational%20Structure.md) and [FOM-001 §4.4](../op-manual.md#44-roles-responsibilities-and-authorities). All management roles at facility level, including shift supervision and Chief Warden, are held by the **Facility Manager**.

### 4.1 CEO, Directors and Other Officers
The CEO, the CFO and the Technical, Operations and Sales Directors are officers (s.144). Each officer must:
- Acquire and keep up-to-date knowledge of OHS matters and of the hazards and risks of thermal processing operations.
- Ensure there are appropriate resources and processes to eliminate or reduce risks. Approve the OHS budget.
- Receive and consider OHS performance reports at least quarterly. Verify that resources and processes are being used.

The **CEO** approves the [OHS Policy](ohs-001-ohs-policy.md) and this procedure.

### 4.2 Operations Director
- Is accountable for OHS performance at the facility and for implementing the OHS management system. Is the Facility Manager's line manager.
- Approves OHS policies, procedures, objectives and significant changes.
- Ensures OHS is part of business planning, purchasing, design and change management (FOM-001 §5.5).
- Chairs the annual management review, including the OHS review.
- Leads by example. Visits the site and talks with workers about safety regularly.
- Is the escalation point for issue resolution ([OHS-003](ohs-003-issue-resolution.md)) and the backup WorkSafe contact.

### 4.3 Facility Manager
- Holds every management role at the facility. Owns the OHS management system day to day: risk register, procedures, inspections, training matrix and records.
- Ensures safe plant, safe systems of work, permits, isolation (LOTO), maintenance and emergency arrangements.
- Plans each shift's work and rosters safely. Holds pre-start meetings. Checks that workers are competent, fit for work, and have the right PPE and permits.
- Responds to hazard and incident reports immediately. Makes the area safe and escalates.
- Is the **Chief Warden**, the **WorkSafe contact** for the site, and the **employer representative** for issue resolution ([OHS-003](ohs-003-issue-resolution.md)).
- Notifies WorkSafe and EPA of incidents, leads investigations, and approves restarts ([OHS-004](ohs-004-incident-reporting-and-investigation.md)).
- Ensures contractors are pre-qualified, inducted and supervised.
- Has the authority to **stop any work** and to remove anyone from site for unsafe behaviour.
- May delegate specific tasks in writing to a competent Operator when not on site (for example warden duties, first response to incidents, hold-point checks). The delegation is recorded in the Training and Competency Matrix (FOM-001 §4.4). Accountability stays with the Facility Manager.

### 4.4 Chief Chemist and Quality Analysts
- **Chief Chemist:** ensures laboratory work is done safely (fume cupboards, sample handling) and owns [OHS-015](ohs-015-chemical-management.md).
- **Quality Analyst (facility):** maintains the hazardous substances register, safety data sheets and labelling. Arranges and performs atmospheric monitoring when the risk assessment requires it (Regs r.166). Reports results to the Facility Manager.

### 4.5 HR Manager
- Manages recruitment, position descriptions (including OHS responsibilities) and the training system and records ([OHS-006](ohs-006-training-and-induction.md)).
- Is the appointed **Return to Work Coordinator** ([OHS-005](ohs-005-injury-management-and-return-to-work.md)) and a **Contact Officer** ([OHS-009](ohs-009-bullying-and-harassment.md)), unless another person is appointed.
- Owns the mental health, bullying and harassment, and drugs and alcohol policies ([OHS-008](ohs-008-mental-health-and-support-program.md), [OHS-009](ohs-009-bullying-and-harassment.md), [OHS-013](ohs-013-drugs-and-alcohol.md)).

### 4.6 Operators and Other Employees (including labour-hire)
Operators are multi-skilled. They perform only the functions they are trained and authorised for in the Training and Competency Matrix, such as Control Panel Operator, Field Operator, Crane Operator (High Risk Work Licence: Regs Part 3.6), First Aid Officer or Warden. All employees must:
- Follow procedures, WIs, permits and instructions. Use PPE correctly.
- Report hazards, incidents, near misses and injuries straight away.
- **Stop work and report** if you believe there is an immediate risk to health or safety.
- Present for work fit and unimpaired by alcohol, drugs or fatigue.
- Treat others with respect. Do not bully, harass or behave violently.
- Take part in consultation, training, drills and inspections.

### 4.7 Health and Safety Representatives (HSRs) and Deputy HSRs
HSRs are elected by the members of a designated work group (DWG) (OHS Act s.43, s.54). Their powers include to (s.58):
- inspect the workplace (after giving notice, or immediately if there is an incident or immediate risk);
- accompany an inspector;
- attend interviews about OHS between employees and the employer (with the employee's consent);
- request a health and safety committee;
- issue a provisional improvement notice (PIN) once trained (s.60);
- direct that work cease where there is an immediate threat, after consulting the employer (s.74).

**Employer obligations to HSRs:** allow initial and annual refresher training of their choice, with pay and costs covered (s.67). Provide facilities, information and time (s.69). Keep an up-to-date list of HSRs and display it (s.71).

### 4.10 Contractors and Visitors
- **Hydroxy Systems Australia** (plant supplier and service provider) is managed as a contractor when its personnel work on site.
- **Contractors:** must be pre-qualified (insurances, licences, safe work method statements for high risk work), complete the site induction, follow permits and site rules, and report incidents. They keep their own duties as employers.
- **Visitors:** sign in, follow the escort's instructions, and wear the PPE issued.

## 5. Accountability
Every position description includes OHS responsibilities. OHS performance is part of manager performance reviews. Lead indicators (for example inspections completed, training current, actions closed on time) are reported monthly to the Operations Director (FOM-001 §8.1).
