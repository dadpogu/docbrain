## 1. Purpose
This register lists the controlled OHS policies and procedures that support the [Integrated Operations Manual FOM-001](../op-manual.md). These documents extend the IMS so that it covers occupational health and safety under the Victorian *Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004* (OHS Act). They are structured so that the IMS can later be aligned with ISO 45001:2018.
