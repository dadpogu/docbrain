## 1. Purpose
To resolve health and safety issues quickly, fairly and in consultation, in line with OHS Act 2004 s.73 and OHS Regulations 2017 Part 2.2.

## 2. Scope
Covers any health or safety issue, physical or psychological, that arises at the facility or from Reclaim Resources' work. Raise hazards that can be fixed immediately through normal hazard reporting ([OHS-004](ohs-004-incident-reporting-and-investigation.md)). Use this procedure when an issue is not resolved that way or needs agreement. Bullying and harassment complaints about individual behaviour follow [OHS-009](ohs-009-bullying-and-harassment.md).

## 3. Parties
| Party | Who |
|-------|-----|
| **Employer representative** (r.23(1)) | **Facility Manager [Name TBC]**, who has enough seniority and competence to resolve OHS issues (s.73(2)). If the Facility Manager is unavailable, the most senior manager present at that part of the workplace acts (r.23(2)). |
| **Employee representative** (r.23(3), (4)) | The HSR (or deputy HSR) for the designated work group. If there is no HSR, one or more employees nominated by the affected employees. |
| **Assistance** (r.23(5)) | Either party may get help from a relevant employee or employer organisation (e.g. a union or industry body) at any stage. |

The employer representative's name and position are displayed on the OHS noticeboard, in the appropriate languages (r.23(1)).

## 4. Procedure
```text
Issue arises
   │
   ▼
1. Report ──▶ HSR (or the Facility Manager as employer rep if no HSR)
   │
   ▼
2. Meet as soon as reasonably possible ──▶ agree temporary measures if needed
   │
   ├── Resolved ──▶ 4. Record, communicate, close
   │
   ▼ Not resolved
3. Escalate ──▶ Operations Director joins ──▶ still unresolved ──▶ either party may ask WorkSafe for an inspector (s.75)
   │
   └── Immediate threat at any stage ──▶ Cease work (s.74)
```

| Step | Action | Timeframe |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 1. Report | The employee reports the issue to their **HSR**. If there is no HSR, they report to the **Facility Manager (the employer representative)** (r.24(2)). The employee may take reasonable steps to report it, including leaving their work area (r.24(3)). They may also report to anyone else (r.24(4)). | Immediately |
| 2. Meet and try to resolve | The employer representative and the HSR (or nominated employees, or the affected employees) meet (r.25(1)). They take into account: the number and location of employees affected; whether temporary measures are possible or desirable; how long a permanent fix will take; and who will carry out and oversee the agreed actions (r.25(2)). | As soon as reasonably possible. Target: within **1 working day** (same shift if serious) |
| 3. Escalate | If the issue is not resolved, the Operations Director joins the discussion. If it is still unresolved after reasonable efforts, either party may ask WorkSafe to send an inspector (s.75, WorkSafe 13 23 60). The inspector's decision on the issue must be followed, unless it is reviewed. | Target: within **5 working days** |
| 4. Record and communicate | If any party asks, the employer puts the issue and its resolution in writing to the satisfaction of all parties (r.25(3)). The agreement is brought to the attention of affected employees and sent to the H&S committee, if there is one (r.25(4)). Use the Appendix A record. | Within **2 working days** of resolution |

### 4.1 Immediate Threat: Cease Work (OHS Act s.74)
If the issue involves an **immediate threat** to anyone's health or safety, and following the steps above is not appropriate, the **employer or the HSR may direct that the work stop**, after consulting each other.
- Make the area safe: isolate the plant or use [WI-007 Emergency Shutdown](../../work-instructions/w07-emergency-shutdown.md) if needed.
- Employees affected can be assigned suitable alternative work. They keep their normal pay.
- Either party may ask for a WorkSafe inspector.
- **Any employee may stop their own work** if they reasonably believe it is unsafe. They must report it to the Facility Manager straight away.

### 4.2 HSR Powers
A trained HSR may issue a **Provisional Improvement Notice (PIN)** if they believe a person is breaching the OHS Act or Regulations (s.60). The employer must comply, or ask for an inspector within 7 days. Do not remove or alter a displayed PIN.

### 4.3 No Discrimination
Nobody will be treated unfavourably for raising an issue, acting as an HSR, or taking part in issue resolution (OHS Act s.76).
