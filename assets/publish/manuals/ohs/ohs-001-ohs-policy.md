## Policy Statement
Reclaim Resources recovers resources from plastic waste using thermal depolymerisation. Our work involves high temperatures, flammable gases and liquids, heavy lifting, hazardous substances, shift work and interaction with suppliers, transporters and the community. **We believe every injury and work-related illness, physical or psychological, is preventable. Nobody's health or safety is worth risking for production.**

So far as is reasonably practicable, we will provide and maintain a working environment that is safe and without risks to health for our employees, contractors, labour-hire workers, visitors and the community (OHS Act 2004 s.21, s.23 and s.26).

## Our Commitments
To meet this policy we will:

1. **Comply with the law.** We will meet or exceed the *Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004*, the *OHS Regulations 2017*, the *OHS (Psychological Health) Regulations 2025*, the *Dangerous Goods Act 1985*, the *Workplace Injury Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2013*, applicable compliance codes, and our EPA licence conditions.
2. **Manage risk systematically.** We will identify hazards, assess risks and control them using the **hierarchy of control**, with the first priority being to eliminate the risk. This applies equally to physical and psychosocial hazards.
3. **Consult.** We will consult genuinely with employees, Health and Safety Representatives (HSRs) and contractors on matters that affect their health and safety, and resolve issues promptly and fairly (OHS Act s.35, s.73).
4. **Provide competent people and resources.** We will provide the information, instruction, training, supervision, equipment, PPE and facilities needed to work safely, including licences for high risk work.
5. **Protect psychological health.** We will prevent bullying, harassment (including sexual harassment), occupational violence, gendered violence and excessive fatigue, and provide access to confidential support.
6. **Be fit for work.** We will maintain a workplace free from impairment by alcohol, drugs or fatigue.
7. **Prepare for emergencies.** We will maintain emergency procedures, first aid and drills suited to a thermal processing facility.
8. **Report and learn.** We will encourage reporting of hazards, incidents and near misses without blame. We will investigate to find root causes and share what we learn. We will notify WorkSafe Victoria of notifiable incidents immediately.
9. **Support recovery.** We will support injured workers to recover at work or return to work safely and early.
10. **Improve continually.** We will set measurable OHS objectives, monitor performance, audit our system and review this policy annually.

## Responsibilities in Brief
Detailed roles are set out in [OHS-002 OHS Roles and Responsibilities](ohs-002-ohs-roles-and-responsibilities.md).

- **Management** must provide leadership, resources and a safe system of work, and must hold themselves accountable for OHS performance.
- **The Facility Manager** holds all management roles at the facility and must make sure work is planned and done safely, and must act on hazards and reports.
- **Every worker** must take reasonable care for their own health and safety and that of others, follow instructions and procedures, use the PPE provided, report hazards and incidents, and **stop work that they believe is unsafe** (OHS Act s.25).
- **Contractors and visitors** must follow site rules and the instructions they are given.

## Communication
This policy is displayed at the site entrance, in the crib room and at the control room. It is explained at induction, available on the IMS document system, and given to contractors before they start work.
