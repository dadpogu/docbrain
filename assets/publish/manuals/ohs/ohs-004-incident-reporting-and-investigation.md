## 1. Purpose
To make sure that all incidents, near misses and hazards are reported, that WorkSafe Victoria (and EPA, where relevant) is notified as the law requires, and that incidents are investigated so the causes are fixed and do not happen again.

## 2. Scope
Covers all incidents involving employees, contractors, visitors or the public at the facility, or arising from Reclaim Resources' work. This includes injuries, illnesses, psychological injuries, dangerous occurrences, near misses, hazards, property damage, and environmental incidents. Plant emergency response is in [WI-007](../../work-instructions/w07-emergency-shutdown.md).

## 3. Definitions
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Incident** | Any unplanned event that caused, or could have caused, injury, illness, damage or environmental harm. |
| **Near miss** | An incident in which nobody was hurt and nothing was damaged, but which could have caused harm. |
| **Hazard** | Anything with the potential to cause harm, including psychosocial hazards. |
| **Notifiable incident** | An incident described in OHS Act s.37 (see Section 5). |

## 4. Reporting Procedure (all incidents)
| Step | Who | Action | When |
|------|-----|--------|------|
| 1 | Anyone | **Make the area safe** if you can do so without risk. Get first aid ([OHS-014](ohs-014-first-aid-arrangements.md)). Call **000** for an emergency. | Immediately |
| 2 | Person involved / witness | Tell the **Facility Manager** verbally (or, if the Facility Manager is off site, the delegated Operator on shift, who tells the Facility Manager by phone). | Immediately |
| 3 | Facility Manager | Assess whether the incident might be **notifiable** (Section 5). If so, notify straight away and make sure **the scene is not disturbed**. | Immediately |
| 4 | Person involved / Facility Manager | Complete Part 1 of [F-004 Incident, Near Miss & Nonconformance Report](../../forms/f04-incident-nonconformance-report.md). For injuries, also record it in the **Register of Injuries** (required under the *Workplace Injury Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2013*). | Before the end of the shift |
| 5 | Facility Manager | Review the report. Confirm notification obligations. Assign the investigation level (Section 6). | Within 1 working day |

> Hazards that are not incidents are also reported on F-004, or raised with the Facility Manager or HSR. Psychosocial hazards and bullying can be reported confidentially ([OHS-009](ohs-009-bullying-and-harassment.md), [OHS-010](ohs-010-psychosocial-risk-management.md)).

## 5. Notifiable Incidents (OHS Act s.37–39)
### 5.1 What Must Be Notified
**(a) Incidents resulting in:**
- death of a person;
- a person needing medical treatment within 48 hours of exposure to a substance;
- a person needing immediate treatment as an in-patient in a hospital;
- a person needing immediate medical treatment for amputation, serious head injury, serious eye injury, de-gloving or scalping, **electric shock**, spinal injury, loss of a bodily function, or serious lacerations.

**(b) Dangerous occurrences** that expose a person to a serious risk from an immediate or imminent exposure to:
- an uncontrolled escape, spillage or leakage of any substance, including dangerous goods (**e.g. a hydrocarbon or gas release from the process**);
- an implosion, **explosion or fire**;
- electric shock;
- the fall or release from height of any plant, substance or thing (**e.g. a dropped pot, lid or bulk bag**);
- the collapse, overturning, failure or malfunction of, or damage to, prescribed or registered plant (**e.g. cranes, pressure equipment [TBC confirm registration status]**);
- collapse of a building or structure.

**(c)** Any other incident or illness prescribed by the regulations.

### 5.2 How to Notify
| Step | Requirement | Who |
|------|-------------|-----|
| 1 | **Notify WorkSafe immediately** after becoming aware: phone **13 23 60** (s.38(1)). Record the time and reference number. | Facility Manager (or the Operations Director, or the most senior person on site if they are unavailable) |
| 2 | Send a **written record within 48 hours** using the WorkSafe approved form (online incident notification) (s.38(3)). | Facility Manager |
| 3 | **Preserve the site:** do not disturb it until an inspector arrives or gives a direction (s.39). You may disturb the site **only** to protect someone's health or safety, help an injured person, or take essential action to make the site safe or prevent a further incident. | Facility Manager (or delegated Operator until the Facility Manager arrives) |
| 4 | Keep a copy of the record for **at least 5 years**. Make it available to inspectors, injured persons, and HSRs (s.38(4)). | Facility Manager |
| 5 | Also check and meet **EPA** notification duties for pollution incidents (licence PPL000300013, EPA 1300 372 842), and **Energy Safe Victoria** duties for serious electrical incidents ([OHS-016](ohs-016-electrical-safety.md)). | Facility Manager |

**Never delay a notification while you investigate.**

## 6. Investigation
### 6.1 Investigation Level
| Actual / potential severity | Examples | Level | Lead | Complete within |
|-----------------------------|----------|-------|------|-----------------|
| Notifiable, or potential for fatality or permanent injury | Fire, gas release, dropped load, electric shock, hospitalisation | **Level 3: formal root cause analysis** (e.g. ICAM or TapRooT) | Facility Manager + HSR + independent person | 20 working days |
| Medical treatment, lost time, or a significant near miss | Burn needing a doctor, chemical splash to the eye | **Level 2: structured investigation** (5-Whys, fishbone) | Facility Manager + HSR | 10 working days |
| First aid only, minor near miss, or hazard | Minor cut, slip without injury | **Level 1: Facility Manager review** | Facility Manager | 5 working days |

### 6.2 Investigation Steps
1. **Secure the evidence:** photos, CCTV, control system data logs (F-007), equipment, permits and records. Respect the s.39 site preservation rules.
2. **Interview people** involved and witnesses separately, as soon as practical. Be supportive and non-blaming. For traumatic events, use a trauma-informed approach and offer the EAP ([OHS-008](ohs-008-mental-health-and-support-program.md)).
3. **Establish the facts:** sequence of events, conditions, actions, and how existing controls performed.
4. **Find the root causes.** Look past "human error" to systems: design, procedures, training, supervision, workload, fatigue, and organisational factors.
5. **Decide corrective actions using the hierarchy of control**: eliminate, then substitute, isolate or engineer, then administrative controls, then PPE.
6. **Consult** the HSR and affected workers on the findings and actions (s.35).
7. **Record** the investigation in F-004 Parts 3–6, and track actions to closure.
8. **Verify effectiveness:** check that the actions worked and that there has been no recurrence.
9. **Share what was learned** through toolbox talks, safety alerts, and updates to WIs and the risk register.

## 7. Monitoring and Reporting
- Monthly report to the Operations Director: incidents by type and severity, LTIFR / TRIFR, near-miss reporting rate, overdue actions.
- Quarterly report to the CEO, Directors and other officers ([OHS-002](ohs-002-ohs-roles-and-responsibilities.md) §4.1).
- Trend analysis at management review (FOM-001 §8.5).
