## 1. Purpose
To control the risks from hazardous substances and dangerous goods that are bought, used, stored, **produced** or disposed of at the facility. This procedure meets the OHS Regulations 2017 Part 4.1 and the *Dangerous Goods Act 1985* / Dangerous Goods (Storage and Handling) Regulations **[confirm the current version]**.

## 2. Scope
Covers purchased chemicals (sealants, greases, solvents, laboratory reagents, cleaning agents, fuel for the thermal oxidiser, compressed gases); **process products and intermediates** (H1, H2, H3, H4 hydrocarbon fractions, process water, non-condensable gas); **by-products and wastes** (char, filter residues, spent activated carbon, oily wastes); and **substances generated** by the process (CO, H₂S, VOCs, hydrocarbon vapour, char dust).

## 4. Procedure
### 4.1 Purchasing and Approval of New Chemicals
1. Before buying, the requester submits a request with the SDS to the Quality Analyst.
2. The Quality Analyst assesses the chemical (with the Chief Chemist where needed): can it be **eliminated**, or replaced with something less hazardous? Is it compatible with stored materials and with the process? Are any environmental restrictions or DG quantities affected?
3. Once approved, the chemical is added to the register **before** it arrives.

### 4.2 Safety Data Sheets (r.155–157)
- Get the current SDS (less than 5 years old) from the supplier before or at first supply.
- Keep it **readily accessible** to workers at the point of use (SDS folders at the control room, laboratory and tank farm) and electronically.
- Do not alter supplier SDSs.
- **As the manufacturer of H1–H4 and char, Reclaim Resources must prepare SDSs** for these products, covering their composition and hazards (r.144–148), before supplying them to customers. **[TBC: engage a competent person.]**

### 4.3 Labelling (r.158–161)
- Label every container with the product identifier and hazard information (GHS). This includes decanted containers, sample bottles, process tanks, char containers and waste drums.
- Identify hazardous substances in pipework and plant: label pipes and tanks with contents and flow direction (r.160).
- Label waste containers with their contents (r.161).

### 4.4 Register (r.162)
Keep a **hazardous substances register** containing: a list of all hazardous substances at the workplace and their current SDSs. Also keep the **DG manifest and placarding** required for quantities over the DG thresholds **[TBC]**.

### 4.5 Risk Assessment and Control (r.163, hierarchy)
For each substance and task, assess exposure and control it in this order:
1. **Eliminate**: e.g. avoid unnecessary solvents.
2. **Substitute**: e.g. use a low-VOC sealant or an aqueous cleaner.
3. **Isolate**: closed transfer systems, the enclosed cleaning bay, bunded tank farm, flammable liquids cabinets.
4. **Engineering controls**: local exhaust ventilation, extraction to the activated carbon system, earthing and bonding, Ex-rated equipment in hazardous areas, gas detection, interlocks.
5. **Administrative controls**: WIs, permits, minimum quantities, limits on exposure time, hygiene (no eating in process areas; wash hands).
6. **PPE**: chemical-resistant gloves, goggles and face shields, FR clothing, respirators (fit-tested), chosen from the SDS.

### 4.6 Hazardous Area Classification
Complete a **hazardous area classification** (AS/NZS 60079.10.1) for the tank farm, condensers, filtration area, transfer points and H1 storage. Only use equipment rated for the zone **[TBC – engage a competent person]**.

### 4.7 Exposure Standards and Monitoring (r.165–168)
- Do not exceed the workplace exposure standards (Safe Work Australia WES, transitioning to WEL), e.g. for benzene, CO, H₂S and respirable dust.
- **Atmospheric monitoring** is required where there is uncertainty about whether exposure standards are exceeded. Plan an initial occupational hygiene survey **[TBC]** covering benzene and VOCs during transfers and lid opening, CO, PAHs, and char dust.
- Give results to the workers who may be exposed as soon as reasonably possible (r.167). Keep records for **30 years** (r.168).

### 4.8 Health Monitoring (r.169–171)
Required when a substance listed in Schedule 9 of the OHS Regulations is used and there is a significant risk to health (e.g. **benzene**, PAHs **[TBC – confirm with an occupational hygienist]**), or where valid techniques exist to detect health effects. Use a registered medical practitioner. Keep results confidential and retain them for **30 years** (r.171).

### 4.9 Storage and Handling
- Segregate incompatible materials (oxidisers away from flammables) using the DG segregation chart.
- Store flammable liquids in approved cabinets (AS 1940) or bunded tanks. Bund capacity must be ≥ 110 % of the largest tank, or 25 % of the total, whichever is greater **[TBC per AS 1940 and EPA guidance]**.
- Control ignition sources: no smoking, hot work permits, Ex-rated equipment, earthing.
- Keep the minimum practical quantities in work areas.
- Keep a spill kit appropriate for hydrocarbons at every storage and transfer point.

### 4.10 Spills and Emergencies
Follow the spill response in the Emergency Plan and [WI-007](../../work-instructions/w07-emergency-shutdown.md). Notify WorkSafe of uncontrolled escapes of dangerous goods that create a serious risk (OHS Act s.37(2)(a)), and notify EPA of pollution incidents. Placarding and the emergency information container (manifest box) are for the fire brigade **[TBC – check if required]**.

### 4.11 Disposal
Dispose of chemical waste through licensed receivers and record it in [F-005 Waste Tracking Register](../../forms/f05-waste-tracking-register.md).

## 5. Training
Training for all workers who handle chemicals covers: reading SDSs and labels, hazards of H1–H4 and char, PPE, spill response and earthing. Refresh every 2 years ([OHS-006](ohs-006-training-and-induction.md)).

## 6. Review (r.164)
Review controls: before a change to systems of work; when health monitoring shows adverse effects; after a notifiable incident involving a hazardous substance; when controls are not working; and at an HSR's request.
