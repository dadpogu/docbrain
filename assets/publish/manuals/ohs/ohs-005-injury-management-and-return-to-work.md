## 1. Purpose
To support workers with a work-related injury or illness, physical or psychological, to recover and to stay at work or return to work safely and as early as possible. It also sets out how Reclaim Resources meets its obligations under the *Workplace Injury Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2013* (WIRC Act).

## 2. Scope
Covers all workers of Reclaim Resources who have a work-related injury or illness. Non-work-related injuries are managed with the same supportive approach where reasonable, but outside the WorkCover scheme.

## 3. Our Commitment
- Early, supportive contact with injured workers.
- Suitable or pre-injury employment provided, so far as reasonably practicable.
- Return to work planning done in consultation with the worker, their treating practitioner and the WorkSafe agent.
- Personal and health information kept confidential (*Health Records Act 2001*, *Privacy and Data Protection Act 2014*).
- No discrimination against a worker for making a claim.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Immediately After an Injury
| Step | Action | Who | Timeframe |
|------|--------|-----|-----------|
| 1 | Give first aid. Arrange medical treatment or transport. Call 000 if needed. | First Aid Officer / Facility Manager | Immediately |
| 2 | Report the incident (F-004). Notify WorkSafe if it is notifiable ([OHS-004](ohs-004-incident-reporting-and-investigation.md)). | Facility Manager | Immediately |
| 3 | Record the injury in the **Register of Injuries**. | Facility Manager | Same shift |
| 4 | Contact the worker to check on their welfare and explain the support available, including the EAP ([OHS-008](ohs-008-mental-health-and-support-program.md)). | RTW Coordinator | Within **1 working day** |

### 5.2 Claims
| Step | Requirement | Timeframe |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | Give the worker a *Worker's Injury Claim Form* if they want to claim, and help them with it if needed. | On request |
| 2 | When you receive a claim, give the worker written acknowledgement (a copy of the signed form), complete the employer section, and record the date received. | On receipt |
| 3 | **Claims that include a mental injury:** forward Part A (early notification) to the WorkSafe agent. | Within **3 business days** of receipt |
| 4 | Forward claims for **weekly payments** (with the certificate of capacity), claims over the employer excess, claims where liability is disputed, claims with a cease-work date, and impairment or death claims to the WorkSafe agent. | Within **10 calendar days** of receipt |
| 5 | Minor medical-only claims under the employer excess where liability is not disputed may be reported quarterly. | Within 14 days after the end of the quarter |
| 6 | **Employer excess:** the employer pays the first **10 days** of weekly payments, and medical and like expenses up to the indexed excess (shown as **$903** for 2026/27; confirm). | — |

Late lodgement can lead to penalties and extra liability under the WIRC Act.

### 5.3 Return to Work Planning (WIRC Act Part 4)
Return to work obligations apply for the employer's obligation period. This is generally **52 weeks of incapacity**; confirm with the agent.

1. **Plan for return to work** from when the employer becomes aware of the incapacity. Get relevant information about the worker's capacity (from the certificate of capacity, and the treating practitioner with the worker's consent).
2. **Consult** the worker, the treating health practitioner (with consent) and any occupational rehabilitation provider about the plan.
3. **Identify suitable employment** that matches the worker's capacity. Suitable employment must be meaningful work and must be safe. Examples at this site: control room monitoring, record review, laboratory support, stores, or training, depending on the restrictions.
4. **Document the arrangements** in writing (WorkSafe *Return to work arrangements* form). Give a copy to the worker and the agent.
5. **Monitor progress.** Review the arrangements at least whenever a new certificate of capacity is issued, or **every [TBC – 2] weeks**.
6. **Pre-injury employment:** offer the worker their pre-injury role once they have capacity for it.
7. Make sure the return to work is **safe**. Consider OHS risks, including psychosocial risks, when designing duties.
8. Keep records of all return to work actions.

### 5.4 Psychological Injury
- Use a trauma-informed, non-blaming approach.
- If the injury relates to workplace behaviour (e.g. bullying), make sure the return to work arrangements do not expose the worker to the same hazard. Review controls under [OHS-010](ohs-010-psychosocial-risk-management.md).
- Offer the EAP and keep communication regular and supportive.

### 5.5 Disputes
Raise concerns with the RTW Coordinator first. Either party may contact the WorkSafe agent. Unresolved disputes about suitable employment may be referred to WorkSafe or the **Accident Compensation Conciliation Service (ACCS)**.
