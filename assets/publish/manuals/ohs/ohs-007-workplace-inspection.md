## 1. Purpose
To find hazards and failed controls before they cause harm, by inspecting the workplace regularly and systematically and following up on the findings.

## 2. Scope
Covers all areas of the facility: receiving and feedstock storage, loading area, kiln and condenser area, tank farm and bunds, cleaning bay and char storage, filtration and centrifuge area, laboratory, workshop, control room, offices, amenities, yard and traffic areas.

## 4. Procedure
1. **Plan:** choose the area and checklist. Invite the HSR. Review previous findings and recent incidents for that area.
2. **Inspect:** walk the area and **talk to the workers** about hazards they have seen. Observe tasks being done. Use the Appendix A checklist as a prompt, not a limit.
3. **Immediate action:** if you find an **immediate risk**, stop the work, make it safe and isolate the hazard. Escalate under [OHS-003 §4.1](ohs-003-issue-resolution.md).
4. **Record:** note each hazard, its location, a risk rating (use the risk matrix: High / Medium / Low), the action, the owner and the due date. Take photos.
5. **Assign actions** using the hierarchy of control. Target close-out times: **High: within 24 h** (make safe immediately), **Medium: within 7 days**, **Low: within 30 days**.
6. **Communicate:** display the summary on the OHS noticeboard. Discuss it at the toolbox meeting and the H&S committee.
7. **Follow up:** the Facility Manager tracks actions to closure. Overdue High actions are escalated to the Operations Director.
8. **Analyse:** trend the findings quarterly and add them to the risk register and management review.
